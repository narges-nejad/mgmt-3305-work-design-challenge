## Instructions

1. Enter your team name and the names of all team members.
2. Complete all six competitive rounds.
3. Discuss each decision with your team before selecting an answer.
4. The activity takes approximately 30–40 minutes.
5. Copy your completion code at the end and submit it to your instructor.

## Topics Covered

- Job design and its objectives
- Efficiency and behavioral approaches
- Specialization
- Job enlargement, rotation, and enrichment
- Motivation and trust
- Teams and successful team building
- Ergonomics
- Quality of work life
- Working conditions, safety, and ethics
